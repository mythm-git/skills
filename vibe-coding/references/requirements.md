# Requirements and Planning

For medium/high-risk work, define outcome, users, constraints, acceptance criteria, affected components, nonfunctional needs, tests, migration/rollback needs, and assumptions. Inspect before proposing files or behavior. Prefer a short plan for low-risk work; do not overplan.

Compatibility, accessibility, observability, privacy, latency, and operational ownership are requirements when the context makes them relevant. Flag unknown material requirements instead of inventing them.
