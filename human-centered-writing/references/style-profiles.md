# Style Profiles

## Casual and social

Use the shortest structure that communicates the point. Contractions, fragments, and direct questions are acceptable when natural. Do not add hashtags, emojis, or a closing prompt unless requested.

## Professional email and applications

State the purpose early. Keep politeness credible and specific. For applications, ground every claim in supplied qualifications and the role; never invent achievements, motivations, or experience.

## Academic, scientific, and technical

Preserve discipline-specific terminology, citations, logical qualification, and reproducible syntax. Formality is not a defect. Do not simplify code, commands, formulas, or precise definitions merely to sound conversational.

## Educational and marketing

Explain through useful examples and clear sequence. Marketing should name supported benefits and limits, not rely on hype or generic superlatives.

## English

Match the requested regional spelling and formality. Contractions can improve a conversational register but do not belong in every context.

## German

Choose **du** or **Sie** deliberately and use it consistently. Prefer idiomatic German order and collocations over literal English translation. Preserve learner-level language unless the user asks for correction; do not introduce intentional mistakes.

## Other languages

Retain the source language, regional spelling, and legitimate cultural markers. Do not translate, normalize dialect, or erase code-switching unless asked.
